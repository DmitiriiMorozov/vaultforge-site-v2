Obsidian mini-course template

Build a short course in five linked notes. Includes a worked exercise, an answer key and a blank lesson in English and Russian.

Download template (.zip)

Try the lesson

A customer ordered 900 units of currency worth of goods and paid 600. Can you find their missing payment when the orders and payments are sorted differently? Work through the three orders in the lesson, then open the answer key.

Use it in Obsidian

  1. Extract the archive and open the en or ru folder as a vault.
  2. Open “Start here” or “Начните здесь” and follow the links to the exercise.
  3. Duplicate the lesson template and replace the exercise and answer with your own.

Для русской версии откройте папку ru в Obsidian и начните с заметки «Начните здесь».
